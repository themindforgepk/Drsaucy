Our Story

Every burger starts with the sauce.

Doctor Saucy began in Gujranwala — the city of wrestlers — with one simple idea: big burgers, honest prices, and a sauce so good people would talk about it. Today our kitchen at Al Hameed Chowk, near D Ground, is one of People's Colony's busiest late-night tables.

Our kitchen team treats each order like a prescription: fillets marinated in-house, patties pressed fresh, buns toasted to order and every plate finished with a signature sauce made in our own kitchen.
